Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Omakase Automation ("we," "us") provides AI-driven sales and marketing automation for small and mid-size businesses, including automated responses on our own Instagram account (@pasha_gear).

What we collect

When you comment a keyword (such as "AUDIT") on one of our Instagram posts, our system reads the text of that comment and your Instagram user ID through Instagram's official Graph API, solely to detect the keyword and send you a private reply.

How we use it

  • To detect a matching keyword in a public comment on our own posts.
  • To send you a single private reply (via Instagram's official Private Replies API) containing the resource you asked for — currently a free PDF, "The Automation Audit."

We do not use comment or message content for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with third parties.

What we don't do

  • We don't store your comment text or message history beyond what's needed to process the single reply.
  • We don't message you unless you first comment a matching keyword on our post.
  • We don't access your DMs, followers list, or any other account data.

Data retention

Comment and reply logs are kept only for operational debugging and are not retained beyond what's necessary to confirm delivery.

Your choices

You can stop receiving replies at any time by not commenting the keyword again, or by messaging us to ask that any record be deleted.
